◆ Contacts Migration

Move your contacts to a new account — safely.

Back up, combine multiple exports, review & merge duplicates, verify everything arrived, then delete the old copies. Fully offline — nothing leaves your computer. macOS, Windows & Linux.

What it does
  • Combine one or more exported .vcf files into one set.
  • Auto‑suggested duplicate detection with an interactive merge — inspect each contact, exclude members, and edit the merged result.
  • Human‑readable backup + verification before anything is deleted.
  • Guided, gated delete of the old copies (macOS automates it; Windows/Linux show manual steps).
