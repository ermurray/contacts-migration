Prepare

Connect your iPhone

Needed only for AirDrop/direct transfer. Using iCloud.com? You can skip this.

Show how to connect
  1. Connect the iPhone with its cable — or put both devices on the same Wi‑Fi for AirDrop.
  2. Unlock the iPhone.
  3. If prompted, tap Trust This Computer and enter your passcode.

Export contacts from your iPhone

Get the old contacts into one .vcf file. Method A is recommended.

Method A — On your iPhone, no sync required recommended
  1. Open the native Contacts app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Lists button in the top‑left corner to view your contact lists.
  3. Touch and hold the list to export (e.g. All Contacts or All iPhone).
  4. Tap Export from the pop‑up menu.
  5. Select the fields to include (phone, email, notes…), then tap Done.
  6. Send it via Mail, Save to Files, or AirDrop to this computer.
  7. Open that .vcf here (email / Files / Downloads), then choose it below.

No Export option? Update to the latest iOS, or use Method B/C.

Method B — iCloud.com (any computer)
  1. Open icloud.com and sign in with the old account ▸ Contacts.
  2. Click a contact, press ⌘A / Ctrl‑A to select all.
  3. ⚙︎ gear (lower‑left) ▸ Export vCard… and save the file.
Method C — Mac Contacts app
  1. Open Contacts, pick the old account/group in the sidebar.
  2. Edit ▸ Select All, then File ▸ Export ▸ Export vCard….

Back up

Saves a copy of all exported contacts (CSV + lossless .vcf) to your Documents folder.

Snapshot current contacts macOS

Records the contacts already on this Mac so old copies can be removed precisely. macOS will ask permission the first time.

Blocked earlier? Click Open Contacts permission settings, enable this app under Automation ▸ Contacts (and Privacy & Security ▸ Contacts), then click Snapshot again.

Import into your NEW account

Put the selected contacts into the new account, using the file this app prepared:

Import file: (complete the Select step first)

  1. System Settings ▸ Contacts ▸ Default Account = your new iCloud account.
  2. Open Contacts ▸ File ▸ Import… ▸ choose the import file above.
  3. Wait for it to finish syncing to iCloud.

Delete the old copies

Locked until you have a backup and a passing verification. On macOS it removes only the pre‑import originals of the selected contacts (after you confirm). On Windows/Linux it shows manual steps.

⚠️ Before changing phone sync — back up everything

The next steps can overwrite the contacts on your iPhone. First make sure you have a safe copy of every contact list/account on the phone:

  1. Repeat the export in Prepare for each list/account (All Contacts, All iPhone, any work/other accounts).
  2. Keep these .vcf files somewhere safe — and you already have this app's backup of what you migrated.

Toggling iCloud Contacts off and choosing “Delete from My iPhone” removes them from the phone. Don't do it until your backups are confirmed.

Sync the new contacts to your iPhone

Make the phone match what's now in the new iCloud account.

  1. On the iPhone: Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Contacts = On, signed into the new account. Changes from the Mac sync automatically.
  2. Remove the old account so its copies leave the phone: Settings ▸ Contacts ▸ Accounts ▸ [old Exchange account] ▸ Delete Account.
Force the phone to match the desktop (desktop “overwrites” phone)
  1. Confirm your backups first (card above).
  2. iPhone: Settings ▸ [name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Contacts → turn Off → choose Delete from My iPhone.
  3. Turn Contacts back On → the phone re‑downloads from iCloud, which now matches your desktop.

This replaces the phone's local contacts with the iCloud/desktop set — only safe once your backups exist.

🎉 All done

Your selected contacts now live in the new account, you have a full backup, and the old copies are gone.
